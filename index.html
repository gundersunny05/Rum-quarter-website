What we do

We get rum brands in front of the people who pour it.

The Rumah is a rum ambassador at heart. The Rum Quarter is how we turn that into a platform: one shared booth on the world's leading bar shows, full brand promotion on this site, and the on-the-ground activations and distribution introductions that help a brand actually grow in Europe.

Train

Trainings & masterclasses for trade and consumers. Educating bartenders, slijterijen and venues on rum and sugarcane categories.

Activate

Brand ambassadorships across the Netherlands and Europe. Guest shifts, in-store events, brand activations, organised and hosted.

Represent

At trade shows: a cheaper alternative to a full booth. We man the stand, scan visitors, present and represent the brand.

Connect

We bring brands, bartenders, importers and venues together. Our network is your network: introductions, distribution leads, partnerships.

How we work with brands

Three ways to grow with The Rum Quarter.

Whether you're a distiller looking for European distribution, or a venue that wants its rum program to actually sell, we plug in at the level you need.

01

Brand Support

Annual ambassadorships and trade show representation for sugarcane spirit brands. We share one takeover stand at the major European bar shows so you get the exposure of a full booth for a fraction of the cost, plus direct engagement with bartenders, bar owners and importers.

Brand ambassadorship Trade show stand-sharing Lead capture & CRM Brand storytelling Network introductions
02

Activations & Events

Live brand work across the Netherlands and Europe. From retail tastings at the local slijterij to industry masterclasses for bartenders, guest shifts behind partner bars, and fully-produced brand events. Sugarcane spirits only, always hosted by The Rumah.

Retail masterclasses Industry masterclasses Guest shifts Custom brand events
03

Venue Consulting

For bars and restaurants, across any spirit category. Cocktail menu development, staff training and recurring cocktail batch production, so your team pours a consistent, better drink without needing a rum specialist behind every shift.

Menu development Staff training Cocktail batches Ongoing consulting

Why brands join us
