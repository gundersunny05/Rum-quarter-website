What we do

One booth, different brands

Rum never got much attention at bar shows, so in 2024 we did something about it. The Rum Quarter is one big stand where different brands show rum together, as one strong category.

Our visitors work in the industry. They talk to our bartenders, brand ambassadors and brand owners, taste what rum can do for their venue, and learn how different the styles are.

The Rum Quarter comes from The Rumah, our cocktail bar on the Oude Binnenweg in Rotterdam. We want to get everybody involved with the world's most diverse spirit.

On the stand

  • Different brands in one booth
  • A masterclass program
  • Room for tastings and meetings

Brands we worked with

Good company

Rum from the Caribbean, Latin America, the Indian Ocean and further afield.

  • 327 Rum

    Guyana, Guatemala, Panama & Venezuela

    A double aged XO blend of rums from four countries, finished for warm tropical notes. Every bottle is filled by hand.

    Official website
  • Alibi Aged Rum

    Barbados, Trinidad & Jamaica

    Bartender and bar owner Hani Asfdaai developed this rum. It's distilled in Barbados, Trinidad and Tobago, and Jamaica, then blended in the Netherlands.

    Official website
  • Black Tot Rum

    United Kingdom

    The name comes from Black Tot Day, 31 July 1970, when the Royal Navy ended its daily rum ration. The Finest Caribbean Rum blends rum from Guyana, Barbados, Jamaica and Trinidad.

    Official website
  • Clairin

    Haiti

    Clairin is a category of rum rather than one brand. Independent Haitian distilleries make it from sugarcane in small batches, wild fermented and unaged.

  • Doorly's

    Barbados

    Martin Doorly and Co registered the brand in Barbados in 1908. It's made today at the Foursquare Distillery and is best known for its sherry cask finished XO.

  • Dos Maderas

    Jerez, Spain

    This rum ages first in the Caribbean, then travels to Jerez to age further in the sherry casks of Bodegas Williams and Humbert.

    Official website
  • Mates Rum

    Barbados, Dominican Republic & Jamaica

    A blend of three to five year old rums with no added sugar or artificial flavouring. The bottle is 35 percent lighter than a standard design to cut emissions in transport.

  • Paranubes

    Oaxaca, Mexico

    One family has made this pure cane juice rum in the Sierra Mazateca for three generations. They press the cane daily and wild ferment the juice.

    Official website
  • Rhum J.M

    Macouba, Martinique

    AOC Martinique rhum agricole made from fresh estate grown cane juice. Jean-Marie Martin, whose initials give the brand its name, bought the site in 1845.

    Official website
  • Ron Colón Salvadoreño

    El Salvador

    This rum started as a bartender's passion project, made to do something new with the category. The name honours the Colón, the currency El Salvador retired in 2001.

    Official website
  • Ron Cristóbal

    Dominican Republic

    Danish producer 1423 World Class Spirits created this rum, which is aged in the Dominican Republic. The name comes from Cristóbal Colón, Christopher Columbus.

    Official website
  • Ron Cubay

    Villa Clara, Cuba

    Ron Cubay launched in Cuba in 1964 from Santo Domingo in the Villa Clara region, and has been sold internationally since 2010.

  • Sampan

    Hoi An, Vietnam

    Pure cane juice rum from Distillerie d'Indochine near Hoi An. The name refers to the traditional Southeast Asian boat.

  • Savanna

    Réunion Island

    Distillerie de Savanna has made rum at the Bois Rouge estate in Saint André for over 150 years.

    Official website
  • Takamaka

    Mahé, Seychelles

    The d'Offay family has made this rum at the Trois Frères Distillery on Mahé since 2002, from organic sugar cane grown by local cooperatives.

    Official website
  • The Duppy Share

    Jamaica & Barbados

    A blend of rums from Worthy Park Estate and the Foursquare distillery. The name comes from the Caribbean belief that duppies steal the best share of ageing rum.

    Official website
  • William George

    Jamaica & Trinidad

    Four Jamaican pot stilled rums and two Trinidad and Tobago column stilled rums, blended in Amsterdam. Its creators named it after their grandfathers.

    Official website
  • Worthy Park

    Jamaica

    Single estate Jamaican rum from 1,200 feet above sea level, where rum making goes back to 1670. The estate distils it in copper pot stills.

    Official website
