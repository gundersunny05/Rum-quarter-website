Brand spotlight

Six producers, six stories worth telling.

A closer look at partner brands we represent. Each one has a place, a method and a reason to exist. That is the kind of rum we want on the back bar.

Jamaica

Worthy Park Estate

Nestled 1200 feet above sea level, Worthy Park spans over 10000 acres of sugar cane fields and forest. The estate produces award winning Jamaican rum, including its signature Rum-Bar Rum, with traditions dating back to 1670. Sustainability and zero waste production sit at the heart of everything the estate does.

Visit site Contact us
El Salvador

Ron Colón Salvadoreño

RCS started as a bartender's passion project with one aim: to innovate the rum category. The name pays homage to the Colón, El Salvador's historic currency retired in 2001. Like old coins, rum grows more valuable the longer it is aged.

Visit site Contact us
Haiti

Clairin

Unaged, wild fermented sugarcane rum made in small batches by independent Haitian distilleries using traditional pot stills and no additives. Clairin is less a single brand than a whole category, and one of the last truly artisanal rum traditions left in the world.

Learn more Contact us
Réunion Island

Savanna

Distillerie de Savanna has produced rum at the Bois Rouge estate in Saint André for over 150 years. Its rums are shaped by volcanic sugarcane, sea spray and generations of distilling expertise, and are known for precision, intensity and remarkable aromatic depth.

Visit site Contact us
Oaxaca, Mexico

Paranubes

In Rio Tuerto, in the Sierra Mazateca region of Northern Oaxaca, one family has made pure cane juice rum for three generations and over 100 years. Cane from ancestral fields nearly 500 years old is hand harvested and pressed daily, then wild fermented. A true expression of Mexico's cloud forests.

Visit site Contact us
Jerez, Spain

Dos Maderas

Made by Bodegas Williams and Humbert in Jerez through a signature double maturation. The rum is aged first in the Caribbean, then shipped to Spain for further ageing in the same sherry casks the bodega uses for its wines.

Visit site Contact us
